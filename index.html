Privacy Policy

Internet Speed Meter - Taskbar, by ByteAcre · Effective 23 October 2026

Internet Speed Meter - Taskbar ("Internet Speed Meter", "the app") is a Windows app that shows your internet speed on the taskbar and, with Pro, helps you understand your data usage. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: your usage data stays on your PC. ByteAcre does not collect, receive, sell or share any of it. The only things that leave your PC are a speed test you start yourself (see below) and purchases, which the Microsoft Store handles.

Information the app uses on your PC

  • Network traffic totals. The app reads how many bytes your network adapters have sent and received, to show your speed. It does not look at the content of your traffic or the websites you visit.
  • Network names. The names of the networks you connect to (for example your Wi-Fi name), so usage history and data plans can be shown per network.
  • Per-app data usage (Pro). How much data each app used, as recorded by Windows itself.
  • Connection status (Pro). Whether Windows reports that you have internet access, to log outages.

Information stored on your PC

The app saves the following only on your PC, in its own data folder:

  • your settings, including any data plans you set up;
  • hourly upload and download totals per network (usage history);
  • a log of internet outages (start time, duration and network name);
  • your speed test results;
  • a small diagnostic log file, which never leaves your PC unless you choose to send it to us.

This information stays until you delete it or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes it. You can open the folder from the app (Settings › About › Open data folder), and clear the outage log on the Connection log page.

Speed test

When you run a speed test (a Pro feature you start yourself), the app connects to servers of Measurement Lab (M-Lab), an open, non-profit internet measurement project. To measure your connection, M-Lab necessarily receives your IP address and the test traffic. M-Lab publishes every test result, including your IP address and the date and time of the test, as open data for research, and keeps it indefinitely. The app tells you this before your first test, and no test runs unless you start it. The app keeps a copy of your results on your PC, as listed above. See M-Lab's privacy policy.

Purchases

Internet Speed Meter Pro is sold through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft processes the payment and tells the app whether your Microsoft account owns Pro. ByteAcre does not receive your payment details. Microsoft's handling of this information is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send your usage data, network names or any other information to ByteAcre or anyone else.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics or tracking, and it needs no account.
  • It does not access your files, contacts, location, microphone or camera. It only writes a file when you choose to export your usage history, to the place you pick.

Start with Windows

So the speed is on your taskbar when you sign in, the app starts with Windows. You can turn this off in the app's Settings or in Windows Settings › Apps › Startup.

Children

Internet Speed Meter does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
